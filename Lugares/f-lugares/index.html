Who We Are

Built for Kenya's Future

LUGARES Group of Companies is a Kenyan-founded enterprise dedicated to transforming the country's infrastructure landscape through innovative construction solutions and cutting-edge cyber services.

We recognised that Kenya's rapid urbanisation demanded more than just buildings — it demanded smart, connected, and secure infrastructure. From urban road networks and commercial developments to enterprise cybersecurity, we deliver end-to-end solutions that stand the test of time.

Our team combines deep local knowledge with international standards, ensuring every project contributes meaningfully to Kenya's Vision 2030 and the broader goal of sustainable development across East Africa.

We operate with the conviction that infrastructure and technology are inseparable in a modern economy — which is why LUGARES brings both under one roof.
